FAQ

Straight answers.

Is taking part free?

Yes. Creating an account and answering surveys is free.

How are rewards handled?

A reward is only counted after the submission has been verified. There is no automatic credit merely for clicking submit.

Who can see my answers?

Your responses are only visible to authorised researchers reviewing the survey you completed. Other respondents cannot see your answers.

When will surveys be available?

Surveys are published continuously. Sign in to see what's currently available.
